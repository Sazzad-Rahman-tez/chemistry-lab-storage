## MASTER STORAGE GUIDELINES SUMMARY

### Quick Reference by Storage Type

**FLAMMABLE STORAGE:**
- Acetone, Ethanol, Methanol, Hexane, Pentane, Ether, Benzene, Toluene, Xylene
- Store at 5-25°C in metal cabinets with self-closing doors
- Keep minimum 10 feet from oxidizers and heat sources

**CORROSIVE STORAGE:**
- All acids (HCl, H₂SO₄, HNO₃)
- All bases (NaOH, KOH, NH₃)
- Store on lower shelves with secondary containment
- Maintain 15-25°C temperature

**OXIDIZER STORAGE:**
- Hydrogen peroxide, Potassium permanganate, Sodium hypochlorite
- Potassium chlorate, Potassium dichromate
- Segregate from all flammable materials
- Use non-combustible shelving only

**GENERAL CHEMICAL STORAGE:**
- Salts, buffers, analytical reagents
- Standard metal shelving acceptable
- Maintain alphabetical or category organization
- Regular inventory checks required
